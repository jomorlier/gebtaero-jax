# Citing

If you use gebtaero-jax, please cite the works it builds on:

* B. Kirsch, GEBTAero — high-aspect-ratio composite wing nonlinear aeroelastic toolbox,
  <https://github.com/mid2SUPAERO/GEBTAero> (see that repository for the associated publications).
* W. Yu, M. Blair, "GEBT: A general-purpose nonlinear analysis tool for composite beams", *Composite Structures*
  94 (2012) 2677–2689.
* D. H. Hodges, "Geometrically exact, intrinsic theory for dynamics of curved and twisted anisotropic beams",
  *AIAA Journal* 41(6) (2003).
* T. McDonnell, A. Ning, "Aeroelasticity.jl", <https://github.com/byuflowlab/Aeroelasticity.jl>.
* M. J. Patil, D. H. Hodges, C. E. S. Cesnik, "Nonlinear aeroelasticity and flight dynamics of high-altitude
  long-endurance aircraft", *Journal of Aircraft* 38(1) (2001).
* D. A. Peters, S. Karunamoorthy, W.-M. Cao, "Finite state induced flow models, Part I", *Journal of Aircraft*
  32(2) (1995).
* D. H. Hodges, G. A. Pierce, *Introduction to Structural Dynamics and Aeroelasticity*, Cambridge University Press.
